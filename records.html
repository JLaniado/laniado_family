Genealogical Records

Beyond the Chief Rabbis and the branches shown in the Family Tree, family and archival sources document dozens more Laniados — named in sefarim, letters, and above all in the gravestones Chacham David Ben Tzion Laniado copied down on his trips back to Aleppo. For most of these individuals, only a name, a year of passing, and a city survive. They are recorded here as raw genealogical data rather than narrative — a starting point for family members researching their own line.
